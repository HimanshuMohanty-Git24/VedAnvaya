WHAT NOT TO CHANGE, each measured rather than assumed:
  * the lateral series. U+1E3B folds to the lateral sentinel and Devanagari DDA to
    d-with-dot. They are different letters and equating them is a scholarly position on the
    Rigvedic intervocalic alternation, not a normalisation.
  * the homorganic nasals. The anusvara folds to a sentinel while n-dot-above and n-tilde
    stay distinct letters. An edition that writes the homorganic nasal where another writes
    anusvara genuinely differs, and folding them together would erase a variant this layer
    exists to report.
  * numerals. DELETED_MARKS already covers ASCII and Devanagari digits and 0 survive past
    punctuation_normalized on any of the 20,210 verses, so agent 12's inline-verse-number
    defect does not reach this classifier.
