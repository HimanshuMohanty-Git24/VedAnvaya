# Rigveda deterministic knowledge build

Generated by `scripts/generate_knowledge_reports.py`. Manifest
`vedagraph-rigveda-knowledge-deterministic-1.0.0-rc1` over corpus `vedagraph-rigveda-shakala-1.0.0-rc1`.

Source: the commit-pinned digital Rigvedic Anukramaṇī of Akavarapu and Bhattacharya
(2023), repository `https://github.com/mahesh-ak/WSC2023` at commit
`05b5987d6d8d6ec7228926eb68d6a21117c9b1f2`. No language model, embedding, or classifier
participates at any stage; the repository's own devatā classifier and word vectors are
deliberately not ingested. See
[`RIGVEDA_ANUKRAMANI_SOURCE.md`](../architecture/RIGVEDA_ANUKRAMANI_SOURCE.md).

**An assignment is not a textual mention.** `HAS_DEVATA: agniḥ` records that the traditional index assigns Agni as the devatā of that mantra. It does not claim that the word *agni* occurs in the Sanskrit. Literal occurrence will be a separate `MENTIONS_ENTITY` predicate produced by a later deterministic lexical phase.
