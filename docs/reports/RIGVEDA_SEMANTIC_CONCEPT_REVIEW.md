Codex-direct pass did not invent concept labels from translation or pretrained
knowledge, and the reviewed semantic registry remains empty. Consequently there are no
duplicate-concept clusters to merge or report from this run.

grouped; fuzzy similarity must remain a review queue only. Examples such as `Creation`,
