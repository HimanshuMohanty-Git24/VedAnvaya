## Every MISLEADING question, with its exact cause

3 of 100. Each is a case where the obvious answer's **content** is wrong - wrong class membership or a silent zero - not merely thin. Per the frozen document, a caveat is not sufficient to rescue these.

### Q10. Which metals occur in each Veda?

**Cause (one line).** An attested metal is absent from the class the query enumerates: `trapu` (tin) is named at AVS 11.3.8 in the graph's own stored Sanskrit and has no `:Metal` node, so `metals_by_veda` returns five metals and silently reports tin as absent from the corpus.

**Measured rows.** 5 Metal nodes only: gold (hiranya), metal (ayas), silver (rajata), lead (sisa), copper (loha). All 5 carry the Metal label and none is reachable only under the Substance supertype - the 8 non-Metal Substances are soma 1,169 / ghrta 254 / anna 251 / payas 147 / madhu 138 / anjana 18 / parisrut 18 / sura 8, no metals hidden there. BUT the graph's own stored Sanskrit names a sixth metal that has no node: 'trapu' (tin) at AVS 11.3.8, t.text_nfc = 'trapu bhasma haritam varnah puskaram asya gandhah ||8||' - the same brahman-odana passage from which lead (sisa) WAS registered. metals_by_veda therefore returns AV metals as gold 34, lead 4, ayas 3, silver 1 and silently omits tin.

**Query to reproduce.** `metals_by_veda`; `MATCH (w:Metal) RETURN w.entity_key, w.display_label`; `MATCH (p:Passage)-[:HAS_TEXT_VERSION]->(t:TextVersion) WHERE t.text_nfc CONTAINS 'trapu' RETURN p.canonical_citation, p.veda, substring(t.text_nfc,0,80)`; `MATCH (p:Passage)-[:MENTIONS_ENTITY]->(s:Substance) WHERE NOT s:Metal RETURN s.display_label, count(DISTINCT p)`

**Consumer note.** The five counts that ARE returned are correct; only the class roster is wrong. Per-Veda counts are also not normalised for corpus size, though Mantra counts are in the graph.

### Q23. What deity communities emerge from the corpus?

**Cause (one line).** The obvious query for deity communities returns a four-row deity-pair table whose top-ranked and fourth-ranked members are human patrons - `VG:DEVATA:DARBHYAH-RATHAVITIH` and `VG:DEVATA:VASUKRAH`, both `structure='HUMAN'` in the registry - presented in `deity_a`/`deity_b` columns with no caveat on the Devata class, while no community structure exists in the graph at all.

**Measured rows.** Zero nodes in the graph carry a community, louvain or partition property - there is no community structure at all, and entity_centrality_ranked types bridge_centrality as 'NOT_BUILT' for the same reason. The query named for this question, deity_co_occurrence, returns 4 rows from RV-only HAS_DEVATA and its TOP row is 'Rathaviti Darbhya' + 'the Maruts', 3 shared mantras. VG:DEVATA:DARBHYAH-RATHAVITIH has structure='HUMAN' and short_description='A named human patron standing as the addressee of the verse.' Row 4 is 'Indra' + 'Vasukra', and VG:DEVATA:VASUKRAH is likewise structure='HUMAN' ('The rishi Vasukra'). That query's caveat says nothing about the Devata class containing humans.

**Query to reproduce.** `deity_co_occurrence`; `deity_pairs_far_above_chance`; `MATCH (n) WHERE n.community IS NOT NULL OR n.louvain IS NOT NULL OR n.partition IS NOT NULL RETURN count(*)`; `MATCH (d:Devata) WHERE d.display_label CONTAINS 'Rathaviti' OR d.display_label CONTAINS 'Vasukra' RETURN d.entity_key, d.structure, d.short_description`

**Consumer note.** deity_pairs_far_above_chance is the safe alternative: 306 CO_OCCURS_WITH pairs with lift and per-Veda counts over all four Vedas, topped by Mitra-Varuna lift 13.189 (228 RV / 51 non-RV). It is still pairs, not communities.

### The two residuals the implementer recorded - my independent judgement

**`VG:DEVATA:APTRNAH-SURYAH` - CONFIRMED, and slightly worse than recorded.** The node exists with `structure='INDIVIDUAL'`, no `EPITHET_VARIANT_OF` edge, and a `curation_note` that reads *'The label is a qualified Surya, so SOLAR carries over'* - i.e. the registry itself calls it a qualified Surya. `MATCH (d:Devata) WHERE 'SOLAR' IN d.axes AND d.structure='INDIVIDUAL'` returns 6 nodes (APTRNAH-SURYAH, PUSA, SAVITA, SAVITRI-SURYA, SURYAH, VISNUH), so `deities_by_axis` reports SOLAR `individual_deities = 6` when the resolved count is 5. The sharper point is that this **violates the query's own stated guarantee**: its caveat says `individual_deities` *'collapses epithet variants onto their base through EPITHET_VARIANT_OF'*, and for the SOLAR axis it does not. Correcting it would also change the axis ranking (SOLAR would drop below ABSTRACT_PERSONIFICATION and COSMIC_SOVEREIGN at 6). **Verdict impact: none.** No benchmark question's criterion turns on the SOLAR axis count, so I did not flip a verdict for it; it is recorded in the `caveat` field of Q20, Q38 and Q41. It is a defect, not a freeze failure.

**`natural_phenomena_personified` reporting the sun with 3 personifications - CORRECT ASSOCIATION READING, not a misleading count.** My reasoning: all three (Savitr, Surya, Surya-daughter-of-Savitr) are genuinely solar deities associated with `VG:CONCEPT:SURYA-SUN` in the concept lexicon; the three names are printed in the same row as the integer, so the membership is inspectable rather than hidden behind a count; the underlying `DEVATA_ASSOCIATED_WITH` edge is `L1_SOURCE_EXPLICIT` with a concept-lexicon locator; and the caveat states 'Association, not identity. The registry refuses IS_GOD_OF and REPRESENTS by name.' The residual hazard is the **column name**, not the count: the edge asserts association while the column asserts personification, and on a strict reading Savitr is a distinct solar deity rather than a personification of *surya*. That is a naming problem worth fixing, not a wrong answer. Q41 is graded `PARTIALLY_ANSWERABLE`, and the reason it is partial is coverage (9 of 13 NaturalPhenomenon nodes), not this count.

## Defects found that are not benchmark verdicts

1. **Stale caveat on `rivers_mentioned`, contradicted by its own rows.** The caveat states *'There is deliberately no Sarasvati RIVER node'*. `VG:CONCEPT:SARASVATI-RIVER` exists, carries the `:River` label, and is returned by that same query at RV 1 / AV 1 (RV 3.23.4, AVS 6.30.1). Affects Q26 and Q85 as a consumer hazard, not as a verdict.
2. **`trapu` (tin) attested and unregistered.** `AVS 11.3.8` reads *'trapu bhasma haritam varnah puskaram asya gandhah'* in `t.text_nfc`, and there is no Metal node for it, although lead (*sisa*) was registered from the same passage. This is the cause of the Q10 MISLEADING verdict and is also the one externally-verified example of the invisibility Q78 describes.
3. **Human-structure Devata nodes surface in deity columns with no typing.** `VG:DEVATA:DARBHYAH-RATHAVITIH` and `VG:DEVATA:VASUKRAH` both have `structure='HUMAN'` and both appear in `deity_co_occurrence`; `VG:DEVATA:VASUKRAH` also appears as Indra's `co_deities` in `deity_profile`. `deities_by_axis` documents that the Devata class contains human patrons; the queries that surface them do not. 4. **45% of the assertion layer is invisible to predicate traversal.** 2,193 of 4,865 `SemanticAssertion` nodes carry `semantic_predicate` as a property with no `ASSERTION_PREDICATE` edge (2,406 have the edge and no property, 266 have both). 5. **`varuna_profile` returns `co_deities: []`** although Mitra-Varuna is the graph's strongest co-occurrence pair (lift 13.189, 279 shared passages), because `co_deities` is computed on RV-only `HAS_DEVATA` and 'Mitra and Varuna' is a separate composite entity. A reader would conclude Varuna co-occurs with nobody. Flagged on Q44.
