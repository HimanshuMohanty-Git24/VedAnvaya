# Claim–Evidence Ledger

Internal scientific-audit artefact for the VedGraph manuscript. Not intended for
publication with the paper.

Every project-specific claim the manuscript makes is listed here with the evidence
behind it, the kind of evidence it is, and where it appears. The purpose is that a
reader of the paper should never have to take a number on trust, and that a future
editor of the paper can tell which numbers move when the graph moves.

**Frozen at** commit `6bf220c`, branch `main`, clean tree, 20 September 2026.
**Measurement set** `supplementary/fact_freeze.json`, generated by
`figures-src/fact_freeze.py`.

## Evidence kinds

| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **LIVE** | Measured by this paper's own Cypher against the loaded store; in the fact freeze; re-derivable with one command. |
| **REPO** | Recorded in a committed repository artefact (report, receipt, registry, decision record) and quoted here. Not re-measured by this paper unless marked. |
| **CODE** | Read from source: a constant, an enum, a threshold, a policy comment. |
| **EXT** | External scholarship, cited in `references.bib`. |
| **INTERP** | The paper's own reading or argument. Not a measurement. |

Claims marked **[VERIFIED]** were independently re-measured for this paper even
though a repository artefact already stated them.

## 8. Rankings (the Q38 discipline)

The project's worst benchmark failure was a sentence whose figures were all correct
and whose comparative words were not. Every superlative in the paper is therefore
resolved against the live graph, with the runner-up recorded.

| # | Ranking asserted | Winner | Runner-up | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8.1 | Highest undirected parallel coverage | SV, 90.9 % | YV, 34.8 % | `fact_freeze.rankings` **[VERIFIED]** |
| 8.2 | Most frequently dedicated deity (figure selection criterion) | Indra, 2,869 | Agni, 1,988 | `fact_freeze.rankings` **[VERIFIED]** |

Other superlatives in the text are either about the paper's own argument
("the most useful output, for the purposes of this paper"), attributed quotations
from the repository, or accompanied by their measurement in the same sentence.
The claim audit (`figures-src/audit_claims.py`) lists all of them.

## 9. Claims the paper explicitly declines to make

Recorded here because a ledger of positives is not an audit.

- No precision figure for the semantic assertion layer as a whole (§9.3).
- No claim of statistical independence between agents (§5.5, §9.3, Fig 4).
- No claim of one-command reproducibility; a clean clone cannot build a certified
  graph (§8.7, App H).
- No claim that the corpus is "the Vedas" (§3.1, §1.4).
- No claim to novelty for the near-parallel method (§2.5).
- No claim that the reference set is gold (§8.3).
- No cost accounting for the agentic work (§12).
