# VedAnvaya Product V1

VedAnvaya is a reading and research interface for the four Vedic Samhitas. It makes passages,
traditional apparatus, textual relationships and supporting evidence easier to inspect without
presenting the graph as a substitute for traditional study, philology or scholarship.

## What is included

- A reader for four bounded corpora: Rigveda Śākala, Samaveda Kauthuma Ārcika, Yajurveda
  Vājasaneyi Mādhyandina and Atharvaveda Śaunaka.
- Search, passage navigation, entity and deity records, and an evidence-first relationship
  model that distinguishes direct textual data from derived measures and interpretation.
- 16,834 text-verified mantra recordings where a correctly matched source recording exists.
- Ask VedAnvaya: evidence-grounded answers with inspectable citations and explicit refusal
  when the product cannot support a claim.
- Knowledge World, with spatial and planar graph views, Focus and Path modes, and a keyboard
  accessible textual alternative.
- Visualization Lab: seven evidence-led plates, each with its method and stated limits.
- Sources & Methodology, About, canonical SEO metadata, sitemap, robots policy and share cards.

## Corpus scope and known limits

VedAnvaya holds one recension per Veda. The Samaveda is Kauthuma Ārcika only: the gāna corpus
is not included. The Yajurveda is Vājasaneyi Mādhyandina only: the Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda is not
included. The Atharvaveda is Śaunaka only. No separate Brāhmaṇa, Āraṇyaka or Upaniṣad corpus is
included. The Samaveda's `ARANYA` section is a structural division of the modeled Kauthuma Ārcika,
not an independent Āraṇyaka corpus.

Audio coverage is uneven; the Samaveda has no mapped audio. Translation coverage is uneven;
the Samaveda has no released translation in this product. Ask output remains a synthesis layer:
its citations show what the graph records, not an independent scholarly authority.

The graph and ontology are frozen for Product V1. Their reference census is 108,779 nodes and
265,295 relationships. See [PRODUCT_V1_SCOPE.md](PRODUCT_V1_SCOPE.md) for the full scope and
[docs/reports/PHASE_9_RELEASE_BASELINE.md](docs/reports/PHASE_9_RELEASE_BASELINE.md) for the
release inventory.
